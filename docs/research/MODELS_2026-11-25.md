## Ternary Bonsai 2.5 27B (added 2026-11-25, 18:50, the owner's question)

Read on Hugging Face's API and the model card (`prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2.5-27B-GGUF`, created 2026-11-24 16:32 UTC,
Apache-2.0, not gated, base `Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B`) and GitHub's API (llama.cpp). The look at Bonsai 2, the owner's
question of 2026-10-06 (DEVLOG that day; the archived multi-user plan §4.3): not for the farm, PrismML's fork only.
- **Files:** `PTQ1_0` 5.99 GB (1.76 bits a weight) and `PQ2_0` 7.25 GB need PrismML's fork; **`Q2_0` 7.67 GB runs on
  stock llama.cpp** from b11556 (#29600 *Runtime support for Prism Bonsai 2 27B*, merged 2026-10-11 12:39 UTC; b11556
  published 13:31 the same day; our `PINNED_BUILD` b12377 is of 2026-11-14). Each has an `-mtp` file with a draft head
  (+0.24 GB); vision through `mmproj` (0.63 GB in Q8_0).
- **The vendor's numbers** (not ours): agentic coding average 54.2 (Qwen3.8 FP16 60.5, Bonsai 2 37.9, Unsloth
  UD-IQ2_XS 47.8, GSQ IQ2_S 52.5; our default `UD-IQ2_S` is not in the table); SWE-bench Verified 75.6 (FP16 80.6); five
  general categories 82.1 (FP16 84.3). One person, PrismML's runtime, no MTP: 158 tok/s on an RTX PRO 6000
  (`PTQ1_0`); stock llama.cpp on `Q2_0` 109 tok/s against PrismML's 150 on an H200; the draft head 1.5–1.8× on NVIDIA.
  The template thinks at `xhigh` by default; the card samples at temperature 1.0 with `--min-p 0`.
- **For us:** not the class farm (dense, ~9× Qwen3.6-A3B's compute per word; vLLM cannot load these files); a candidate
  to replace the llama.cpp engine's `UD-IQ2_S`, the same base and size, with a draft head `UD-IQ2_S` lacks. Plans ▸
  Next 21. **Not checked:** any of it run; our `UD-IQ2_S` against it on the vendor's benchmarks.

## 3. Fit, by the farm's own Automatic rule (**estimated**)

`vllm.js` `poolGib`: free memory − the model − 4 GiB for vLLM − 9 GiB document reading − 1.5 GiB document search − an 8 %
margin. Then people = pool ÷ (context per person + any fixed state). Two cases where the layout is unsure: a Mamba state
in bf16 or fp32; sliding windows honoured, or "every layer pays" as vLLM 0.33 measured for Gemma 4 26B (37,464 B a
token against the config's 10,240).

**Calibration: the estimator is optimistic.** Qwen3.6 on the whole card: 99 at 64k estimated, 64 measured (×0.65).
Gemma 4 26B: 25 estimated for its 60 GiB pool, 20 measured (×0.8).

| Model (weights GiB) | PRO 6000 as a farm | PRO 6000, whole card | Spark (as a farm) | Spark speed ceiling, one person |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3.6 (20.4) — reference | pool 52: 75 at 64k | 99 (measured 64) | 102 | ~150 tok/s |
| **Nemotron 3.5 Super VL** (69.9) | **pool 2: 5–7 at 64k** | **51–63 at 64k** | 58–72 | ~38 tok/s |
| **Kolibri-1 FP8** (73.5) | **does not fit** | 4–22 at 64k | 5–26 | ~80 tok/s (FP8) |
| Kolibri-1 NVFP4-W4A16, third party (44.1) | 8–43 | 14–68 | 14–72 | ~130 tok/s |
| gpt-oss-120b (60.8), no pictures | 10 | 24 | 26 | ~90 tok/s |
| Qwen3-Coder-Next FP8 (74.9), no pictures | does not fit | 16 | 19 | ~150 tok/s |
| Ripple-65B NVFP4 (53.3), dense, queued | 8 | 16 | 17 | ~7 tok/s |

(The Spark ceiling is 273 GB/s ÷ the bytes of active weights read per token: a ceiling, not a measurement.)

**What the fit says before any measurement:**
1. **The 9 GB kept for document reading decides whether a big model can serve a class on the PRO 6000.** A 70 GB
   model has a 2 GiB pool as the farm runs today, and ~18 GiB without that reserve. Nemotron 3.5 Super VL sees pictures
   itself, so it could read the scanned pages and free the reserve: that is the owner's open question "May vLLM's own
   model read documents?" (plans list ▸ 4. Waiting on the owner, 3).
2. **The Spark holds the big models better than the PRO 6000** (121 GiB against 96), but its memory is ~6× slower: a
   12B-active model like Super VL tops at ~38 tok/s for one person there, and less per person in a crowd.
3. **Kolibri's sliding windows decide between ~4 and ~22 people** on the whole PRO 6000: only a run shows which.

## 4. The RAG check (new, `docs/spike/runs/rag_check.mjs`)

A slice of real text (this repo's DEVLOG) with three made-up facts at 10, 50 and 90 % of its length, at 30k and 60k
tokens, several people at once with their own slices; a fact counts when the reply holds its value. Checked on a stand-in
server (DEVLOG 2026-11-12, 20:07).

### The baseline: Qwen3.6 on the RAG check (**measured**, 2026-11-12, 20:46 to 20:52 by the result files' times)

vLLM 0.33.0 from `~/lol-vllm-033` (production's weights read, not touched), the farm's flags, 64k per person, a 60 GiB pool,
the whole card (no farm running). Results: `docs/spike/results/pro6000-vllm033-qwen36-nvfp4__rag*`.

| Check | Thinking | 30k, 1 / 4 / 8 / 16 people | 60k, 1 / 4 / 8 / 16 people | Slowest answer at 16 people |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain (3 facts each) | off | 3/3 · 12/12 · 24/24 · 48/48 | 3/3 · 12/12 · 24/24 · 48/48 | 7.5 s (30k), 18.1 s (60k) |
| Plain | on | 3/3 · 12/12 · 24/24 · — | 3/3 · 12/12 · 24/24 · — | 7.9 s at 8 (60k) |
| **Hard** (an outdated value planted too, + a comparison) | **off** | 3/4 · — · 22/32 · 48/64 | 3/4 · — · 22/32 · 46/64 | 18.3 s (60k) |
| **Hard** | **on** | 4/4 · — · 31/32 · — | 4/4 · — · 32/32 · — | 25.2 s at 8 (60k) |

- **Finding a planted fact is easy**: Qwen3.6 never missed, at any depth, size or crowd. A model that misses here is out.
- **The hard check separates thinking on and off.** With thinking off, the comparison ("which place has the highest
  current code?") was right 10 times out of 49, and a fact deep in the document with an outdated value earlier on was
  right 33 times out of 48. With thinking on: 71 of 72. Open WebUI's chats think by default, so a person asking about a
  document gets the good answer; the farm's quick tasks and the Computer's boxes (which send thinking off,
  `TASK_MODEL_PARAMS` and `DEFAULT_THINK`) would not, on such a question. The hard check is the one the candidates face.

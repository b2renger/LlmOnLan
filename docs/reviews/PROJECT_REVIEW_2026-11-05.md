context. What changed is in the [DEVLOG](../DEVLOG.md) (2026-11-05 21:31 and after); the next work, in order, is in

  skeptic re-checked every finding and every archive proposal. 330 findings: 147 confirmed, 179 partly (real, with a
  corrected fix), 4 refuted. Applied by ten fixers on disjoint file groups, each diff re-checked. Commits `f855e00` to
  `61ba212`; the DEVLOG entry says what they fixed.

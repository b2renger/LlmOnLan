## Schema (top level)

- `meta`: units, `fit_rule`, the NVLink correction, caveats.
- `hardware[]`, one entry per box:
  - `id`, `kind` (pcie-card / system / cluster), `arch`;
  - memory: `memory_gb_nominal`, `memory_gib_visible`, `memory_type`, `bandwidth_gbs`;
  - compute: `fp4_tflops_sparse|dense`, `fp8_tflops_dense|sparse`, `tensor_note` (which figure is sparse);
  - `power_w`, `price_eur_ttc`, `price_source`, `price_reliability`, `needs_host_pc`, `owned`, `status`, `measured_in_spike`;
  - `budget`, in GiB for weights + KV after runtime: `farm_with_ocr_gib`, `farm_no_ocr_gib` and `single_user_max_gib` on cards; `farm_gib` and `single_user_max_gib` on Sparks;
  - `sources[]`.
  - `dgx-spark-x2` adds `link`, `engines`, `nvidia_model_size_claims`, `throughput_note` and `measured_reports_ids`.
- `models[]`, one entry per model:
  - `id`, `vendor`, `released`, `role`, `params_total_b`, `params_active_b`, `arch{…}`;
  - KV and state: `kv_bytes_per_token_fp8`, `kv_bytes_per_token_bf16`, `kv_constant_bytes_per_request` (sliding/chunked windows), `recurrent_state_bytes` (raw), `vllm_state_charge_bytes` (measured models only), `kv_calc`, `kv_confidence`;
  - `context_native`, `context_max`, `vision`, `tools`, `thinking_toggle`, `license`;
  - `checkpoints[]`: `format`, `repo`, `disk_gb`, `disk_gib`, `bytes_per_param_avg`, plus `loaded_gib_measured` or `resident_gib_est` where known;
  - `engines{}`;
  - `quality[]`: `benchmark`, `score`, `source`, `vendor_reported`, `note`;
  - `spike_quality` (the 28-item gate, measured models only), `reported_runs[]` (community or vendor tok/s with date and source), `aggregate_index` (BenchLM);
  - `fit{"<hw>@<budget>": {vllm_sglang:{…}, llama_cpp:{…}}}`, each with `checkpoint`, `weights_gib`, `kv_room_gib`, `people_by_kv_at_32k_est` and `verdict`.
- `spike_measured`: the RESULTS.md anchors:
  - people per box (`[every turn, steady]` at 32k / 64k / 128k);
  - single-user decode, peak aggregate and cold prefill;
  - KV pool tokens, and vLLM's real capacity at 58 GiB;
  - the PRO 6000 decode and TTFT fits.
- `sources{id: {title, url, date, type}}`: every `source` field points here, or is a Hugging Face model-card URL.

## What this means for "two linked Sparks"

1. **The link is not NVLink.** Two Sparks connect through their ConnectX-7 NICs: one QSFP cable, 200 Gb/s Ethernet with RoCE for NCCL. Each port reaches the SoC through two PCIe Gen5 x4 links, so it shows up as two Linux interfaces.
   - Measured: 196 Gb/s RDMA over both rails. Plain TCP manages only ~16 Gb/s.
   - Up to 3 Sparks can be cabled directly, or 4 through a switch.
   - NVIDIA's size claims: 2 Sparks run models up to ~400B parameters, 4 Sparks up to ~700B.
2. **NVIDIA documents tensor parallel only (TP=2):**
   - vLLM multi-node playbook (container v0.28.0);
   - TensorRT-LLM playbook, which lists Qwen3-235B-A22B NVFP4 as "two Sparks only";
   - SGLang TP=2 and llama.cpp RPC work, but only in community reports.
   - Pipeline parallel is not documented, and no measured pipeline-parallel run was found.
3. **TP=2 buys memory, not speed:**
   - One person: 1.24–1.4× faster, not 2×. Qwen3.8-Flash-Next went from 41.7 to 51.9 tok/s, and the link ran at 0.3% during decode. gpt-oss-120b went from ~55–60 to ~75 tok/s.
   - Many people: gpt-oss-120b on 2 Sparks reached 292 tok/s (vLLM) to 323 tok/s (SGLang) at 32 users. That is a classroom of ~10 tok/s each, below the 15 tok/s bar.
   - If a model fits one Spark, two independent copies serve about 2× the people.

## Not verified, or estimated

### Specs and prices
- **FP8 and dense TFLOPS** are derived as FP4 sparse ÷ 2 ÷ 2.
- **PRO 5500:** TOPS and bus width are unpublished.
- **Max-Q:** 3,511 TOPS is medium reliability.
- **Ada:** that "AI TOPS" means FP8-sparse comes from NVIDIA's convention; it was not re-read today.
- **RTX 5090 FP8 rate** with FP32 accumulate: not verified.
- **Cable price** for two Sparks is estimated (~EUR 80).
- **No education pricing** was found.

### Memory budgets
- Visible memory of the cards not owned is assumed (nominal × 0.99576).
- Spark budgets are judgement calls:
  - farm 84 GiB: the spike's own working point;
  - single user 110 GiB: from community runs that leave 8–13 GB free.
- Not measured: the OS reserve, and whether a Spark at 110 GiB risks the OOM clock latch.

### KV sizes
These are approximations:
- DeepSeek V4/V4.1 compressed attention: ±50%;
- the MiniMax-M3 MSA index cache;
- the GLM-5.3 and GLM-5.3-Flash indexers and k-pool;
- the Qwen3.8-Flash-Next QSA indexer.

The KV size of gpt-oss-120b in fp8 on sm_120/121 is not verified; many deployments run it in bf16.

### Engine support
- vLLM on sm_120/121 is measured only for the 3 spike models.
- Everything else is either "community: yes" with a source, or "not verified":
  - MiMo-V2.6-Flash, Mistral Small 4 and Medium 3.5, Llama 4 and GLM-4.7 on GB10;
  - mainline llama.cpp for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and MiniMax-M3.

### Benchmarks
- Vendor numbers come from different harnesses. Rows run by another vendor are marked `vendor_reported: false`.
- Read off vendor chart images, so ±1 point: GLM-5.3-Flash, Mistral Small 4 and Mistral Medium 3.5.
- MiniMax-M3 numbers come through the BenchLM aggregator.
- The Qwen3-Coder-Next SWE-V 70.6 is taken from the plan, not re-checked.
- No quality numbers for Mistral Large 3.
- The Kimi K3 release date differs between sources: the HF repo is dated 2026-06-13, aggregators say July.

### Other estimates
- **Qwen3.8-Flash-Next "resident with the n-gram table offloaded"** (~76 GiB) is an estimate: file size minus the 51 GB n-gram table.
- **Community tok/s** are single posts with different builds, quants and speculative decoding; several were read from search snippets only (marked).

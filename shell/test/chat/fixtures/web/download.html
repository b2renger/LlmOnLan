Download Quill

The current stable version is Quill 7.2.1, released on 15 September 2026. It is a long-term support (LTS) release, maintained until September 2028.

Windows, macOS and Linux

Quill 7.2.1 runs on Windows 10 and later, macOS 13 and later, and every Linux distribution released since 2022. The installer is 48 MB.

Experimental builds

Experimental builds have the newest changes, but they are unstable and can damage your files. Use them to test Quill 7.3 before its release.

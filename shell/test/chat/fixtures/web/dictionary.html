quill

noun. A quill is a writing tool made from a moulted flight feather, preferably a primary wing feather, of a large bird. Quills were used for writing with ink before the invention of the dip pen.

The hard shaft of a feather, especially the hollow part near the skin of the bird, is also called a quill, and so is each of the sharp hollow spines of a porcupine or a hedgehog.

Synonyms: feather, plume, pen, spine. From Middle English quil, a hollow stem or reed, first recorded in the fifteenth century.

# Inputs

## topic
accessibility in museums for autistic persons

## societal research
## Findings

Museums report that autistic visitors most often leave because of the sound of the room, not
because of the exhibit. Three sites that introduced a quiet hour saw repeat visits rise.

- Predictability matters more than quiet: a published route beats a silent one.
- Staff training changed outcomes more than any building work.

## environmental research
### 1
Lighting is the second most cited barrier. Fluorescent flicker is invisible to most people and
exhausting to some; several sites replaced it during routine maintenance at no extra cost.

### 2
Wayfinding failures concentrate at thresholds: doors, lifts and the first ten metres of a new
room. A floor plan given at the entrance removed most of the reported distress.
